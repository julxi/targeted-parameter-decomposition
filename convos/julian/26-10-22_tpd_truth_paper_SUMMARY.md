## Setup and methods

- **Setup**: tPD of the `down_proj` of Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct layers 11–17 at all statement tokens of the verdict prompt `<statement>\nThe amount of truth in this statement is `, target loss = KL of the verdict digit ("1"/"0"). Main text names the choices, appendix justifies them. Terms: *statement*, *verdict prompt*, *verdict lens*; "stimulus" only ever means the statement [decided: C: comment].
- **Justifications** [verified in the bolder-experiments topic unless tagged]: all tokens and layers 11–17 rest on the causal layer scan (end token alone keeps 82% of the verdict separation; mean-ablating MLPs 11–17 leaves 0.05). **`down_proj` alone misses part of the verdict computation**: the tPD paper decomposes `c_fc`, `down_proj`, `o_proj`, q/k/v [verified: `papers/…/tpd_paper.md`]; ours was narrowed for memory in arm A ("an unvalidated guess", config comment). B1 (attention scan, mean ablation at the statement tokens) [verified: one run, 1,794 test statements; MLP rows reproduce the 26-10-16 scan]: attention 11–14 matters as much as MLP 11–14 (separation share left 0.44 vs 0.38); over 11–17 the MLPs carry more of the separation (0.05 left vs 0.21 for attention), but the KL cost is the same (0.68 vs 0.66). Module set stays `down_proj` 11–17 [decided: Julian, chat transcribed]: MLP 11–17 off already removes the verdict (0.05 left), so every verdict route passes through them; attention is necessary too, in series. So the attention part of the computation is a stated limitation, not a reason for `o_proj` (the agent first proposed `o_proj`, then withdrew it). Limitation either way: `down_proj` shows which MLP hidden directions are read out, not how they are computed.
- **What the verdict lens targets** (B3, Julian's belief-steering request [decided: C: comment]) [verified: one run, 26-10-26]: steering the in-context truth direction at all statement tokens (L17, α = 8) flips the verdict under the verdict prompt (0.975) and the second prompt (0.994); a shuffled-label control flips ≤ 0.08. But in-context recall after the steered statement stays at 0.95–1.00 for cities and elements in every condition, the wider L13–17 edit included. In continuations the model states the flipped digit and then the true fact. Reading [concluded]: the direction acts as a truth tag on the statement that verdict prompts read and fact retrieval ignores, as with the component masks of the steering topic. The paper should say the lens decomposes the verdict (truth assessment), not the model's knowledge. The planned widening to the answer positions was not done: the recall question names the subject again, so editing there would steer the question, not the statement [assumed].
- **Mass-mean vs logistic-regression directions** (A4) [verified: `~/spd_out/26-10-22_tpd_truth_paper/mm_vs_lr.{py,out}`, one run; bare statements, last token, layers 15–19 and 23]: cos(mm, lr) 0.33–0.46 (random ≈ 0.017); lr separates better (test 0.998 vs mm 0.925–0.984); mm is much more stable (split-half cos 0.99 vs 0.65–0.75). So Julian's reason "not very different" does not hold. Justification in the paper: Marks & Tegmark's argument (2023, arXiv:2310.06824: mass-mean probes are "more causally implicated in model outputs" [verified: abstract]) [decided: Julian, chat transcribed in the LOG]; no own steering check.
- **Methods**: plain tPD, + per-layer reconstruction (= Exp 4b/4c), + global orthogonality term (= Exp 4e). The existing runs are not comparable (different positions, LR, steps, PGD, data split; 4e at 4c's LR recovered 0.37 instead of 0.91), so all are redone under one protocol [concluded].

## Selection and evaluation

- **Selection criteria**: Julian's axes (a) components-only KL on target data, (b) Δ-only KL on Pile, (c) Δ-only verdict on target data; Julian rejects the cancellation test as a criterion (close to 4e's training term) and wants a natural one [C: comment]. Proposal [assumed]: tPD's own losses (target KL, Pile, PGD), (a)–(c) with (c) as Δ-only's share of the all-off KL recovered (ideal 0), transfer to a second verdict prompt; cancellation as a diagnostic. **A3 (existing runs, from the records)** [verified: re-read 26-10-26; one seed each; confounded settings]: no criterion separates plain tPD (4a) from all others. Δ only recovers 0.53 in 4a, but also 0.44 in 4b (both at the end token), −0.18 in 4c, ≤ 0 in 4e. Pile KL and PGD separate 4a (0.083, 1.20) from 4b/4c (0.021–0.028, 0.13/1.25), but 4e's screening runs, trained without PGD, have eval PGD 4.4–5.2 and Pile 0.054–0.065. The agent's earlier claim that (c) flags 4a was wrong (marked OUTDATED in the LOG). A3's GPU job is delayed [decided: Julian, chat transcribed]; the between-method criteria are to be decided on C1 (plain tPD at all tokens, PGD on) [assumed: proposal]. All metrics in the **CI-weighted** state [decided: C: comment].
- **Four categories** (Zou et al. 2023, Representation Engineering; confirmed by Julian): correlation, manipulation, termination, recovery. Controls everywhere: untrained decomposition and the data-aligned untrained basis (it swapped verdicts as well as 4e's components). CI probes don't count as correlation evidence (an untrained CI network reads truth equally well); proposed instead: sparsity (few components vs controls at equal k), transfer of single-component selectivity to OOD families, truth-vs-verdict dissociation [assumed: proposals]. Manipulation: show the whole curve over the number of terms (decompositions win only with few terms). **No fixed definition of "mechanism"**: heuristic criteria for "we found something", stated as such, not fixed in advance; be honest that we don't understand what's going on [decided: C: comments].
- Both the ground-truth label and the model's own verdict in every analysis; "4c keeps most functionality in Δ" is enough on Δ; earlier negative results (arm A, UTH, Exp 0–3) in an appendix; low-CI averaging effect only reported as a hypothesis if low-CI clusters keep mattering, no extra experiment [decided: C: comments].
