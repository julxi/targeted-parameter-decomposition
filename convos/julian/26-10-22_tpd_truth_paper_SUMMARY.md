## Setup and methods

- **Setup**: tPD of the `down_proj` of Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct layers 11–17 at all statement tokens of the verdict prompt `<statement>\nThe amount of truth in this statement is `, target loss = KL of the verdict digit ("1"/"0"). Main text names the choices, appendix justifies them. Terms: *statement*, *verdict prompt*, *verdict lens*; "stimulus" only ever means the statement [decided: C: comment].
- **Justifications** [verified in the bolder-experiments topic unless tagged]: all tokens and layers 11–17 rest on the causal layer scan (end token alone keeps 82% of the verdict separation; mean-ablating MLPs 11–17 leaves 0.05). **`down_proj` has no justification yet**: the tPD paper decomposes `c_fc`, `down_proj`, `o_proj`, q/k/v [verified: `papers/…/tpd_paper.md`]; ours was narrowed for memory in arm A ("an unvalidated guess", config comment). Julian wants an attention-ablation scan [decided: C: comment]; if attention matters, adding `o_proj` costs about 19% of a `down_proj` per layer. Limitation either way: `down_proj` shows which MLP hidden directions are read out, not how they are computed. The verdict lens's "belief" premise has counter-evidence (mask-inverted verdicts leave recall intact); Julian agrees and wants a belief-steering experiment with activation steering, plus where the model recovers its information from [decided: C: comment].
- **Mass-mean vs logistic-regression directions** (A4) [verified: `~/spd_out/26-10-22_tpd_truth_paper/mm_vs_lr.{py,out}`, one run; bare statements, last token, layers 15–19 and 23]: cos(mm, lr) 0.33–0.46 (random ≈ 0.017); lr separates better (test 0.998 vs mm 0.925–0.984); mm is much more stable (split-half cos 0.99 vs 0.65–0.75). So Julian's reason "not very different" does not hold. Proposed justification [assumed]: stability, no hyperparameters, and Marks & Tegmark (2023, arXiv:2310.06824): mass-mean probes are "more causally implicated in model outputs" [verified: abstract]. A steering check (lr vs mm) can go into B3.
- **Methods**: plain tPD, + per-layer reconstruction (= Exp 4b/4c), + global orthogonality term (= Exp 4e). The existing runs are not comparable (different positions, LR, steps, PGD, data split; 4e at 4c's LR recovered 0.37 instead of 0.91), so all are redone under one protocol [concluded].

## Selection and evaluation

- **Selection criteria**: Julian's axes (a) components-only KL on target data, (b) Δ-only KL on Pile, (c) Δ-only verdict on target data; Julian rejects the cancellation test as a criterion (close to 4e's training term) and wants a natural one [C: comment]. Proposal [assumed]: tPD's own losses (target KL, Pile, PGD), (a)–(c) with (c) as Δ-only's share of the all-off KL recovered (ideal 0), transfer to a second verdict prompt; cancellation as a diagnostic. **A3 (existing runs, from the records)** [verified: re-read 26-10-26; one seed each; confounded settings]: no criterion separates plain tPD (4a) from all others. Δ only recovers 0.53 in 4a, but also 0.44 in 4b (both at the end token), −0.18 in 4c, ≤ 0 in 4e. Pile KL and PGD separate 4a (0.083, 1.20) from 4b/4c (0.021–0.028, 0.13/1.25), but 4e's screening runs, trained without PGD, have eval PGD 4.4–5.2 and Pile 0.054–0.065. The agent's earlier claim that (c) flags 4a was wrong (marked OUTDATED in the LOG). Proposal [assumed]: decide the between-method criteria on C1 (plain tPD at all tokens, PGD on), no GPU job for A3. All metrics in the **CI-weighted** state [decided: C: comment].
- **Four categories** (Zou et al. 2023, Representation Engineering; confirmed by Julian): correlation, manipulation, termination, recovery. Controls everywhere: untrained decomposition and the data-aligned untrained basis (it swapped verdicts as well as 4e's components). CI probes don't count as correlation evidence (an untrained CI network reads truth equally well); proposed instead: sparsity (few components vs controls at equal k), transfer of single-component selectivity to OOD families, truth-vs-verdict dissociation [assumed: proposals]. Manipulation: show the whole curve over the number of terms (decompositions win only with few terms). **No fixed definition of "mechanism"**: heuristic criteria for "we found something", stated as such, not fixed in advance; be honest that we don't understand what's going on [decided: C: comments].
- Both the ground-truth label and the model's own verdict in every analysis; "4c keeps most functionality in Δ" is enough on Δ; earlier negative results (arm A, UTH, Exp 0–3) in an appendix; low-CI averaging effect only reported as a hypothesis if low-CI clusters keep mattering, no extra experiment [decided: C: comments].
